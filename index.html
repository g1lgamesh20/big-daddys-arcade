HOW THE RANKINGS WORK

Players earn points based on their finishing position on each game: 1st = 100, 2nd = 90, 3rd = 85, then one fewer point for each subsequent place. Each player’s best 1,000 game results count toward their overall total.

These standings are calculated from the available iScored score feed and may differ from iScored’s official rankings.
